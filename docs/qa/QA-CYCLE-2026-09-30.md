## REV-008 — not a product defect

The runner reported **Fail** (`DEF-REV-008`): allocation of 500 to `INV-KHI-2026-003` returned 400 `Allocation to INV-KHI-2026-003 exceeds outstanding`.

That invoice had no open balance. The runner treats a missing or zero outstanding as 999,999 because `0` is falsy (`outstandingPkr || outstanding || 999999`), so it selected a closed invoice. The API correctly rejected it.

Retest of the real case: two sent KHI invoices (`INV-KHI-2026-014`, `INV-KHI-2026-015`), each outstanding **10,000 PKR**. One bulk receipt of **2,000** allocated **1,000 / 1,000**. Both allocations returned **201**. Both invoices became **PartiallyReceived** with outstanding **9,000**. **REV-008 Pass.**
